# Acme Remote Work Handbook

## Expenses

Expenses above 75 euros require pre-approval from your team lead. Receipts must
be submitted within 30 days of purchase. Reimbursement is processed on the 15th
of the following month.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a 1500 euro equipment budget on joining, refreshed every
three years. Monitors and chairs are ordered through the facilities team, not
purchased directly.

## Time off

Annual leave is 25 working days plus public holidays in your country of
residence. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days need two weeks notice.
Unused days expire on 31 March of the following year.

## Security

Full disk encryption is mandatory on every device that touches company data.
Personal devices may not be used for customer data under any circumstances.
